# 02 — Reference titles

References are studied for specific qualities, never copied. We do not
reproduce any title's characters, maps, UI art, music, names or story. Each
entry lists what we **take** and what we **refuse**.

## Core four (director-named)

### REF-1 No Name Village — ambience

- **Take:** soft, warm light and gentle colour; ambient life everywhere (birds,
  insects, drifting particles, swaying grass); a small, intimate village scale;
  quiet moments allowed to breathe.
- **Refuse:** low-detail placeholder sprites; empty screens with no foreground
  interest.

### REF-2 Zenonia 4 — UI/UX and human combat

- **Take:** ornate framed panels with a clear hierarchy; portrait-anchored HUD;
  big tactile action buttons and skill slots; class-identity skill trees;
  combo-driven melee with knockback, damage numbers and flashy but readable skill
  VFX; crowd fights against packs.
- **Refuse:** monetization UI (gacha, energy timers, pop-up offers); clutter
  that buries the play field; stat inflation into the millions.

### REF-3 Pokémon — creature stats

- **Take:** six base stats per species; per-individual variation; natures
  (we call ours **Temperament**); type matchups with clear multipliers; a small
  equipped move set; growth that changes how a creature fights.
- **Refuse:** catch-them-all box collecting (one bonded partner at a time); turn
  menus (our combat is real time); breeding mazes.

### REF-4 Stardew Valley — life sim

- **Take:** villager schedules you learn; gift preferences; heart levels with
  authored heart events; seasonal festivals; the satisfaction of a daily routine.
- **Refuse:** farming as the only progression spine; a calendar so long that
  events are rare (our seasons are 7 days).

## REF-14 How to use references

When proposing a visual or feature, name the reference ID and the specific
quality ("REF-5: face readability at 12 px head height"). "Make it look like
Eastward" is not a usable direction; "two-pixel eye highlights and a one-pixel
mouth that changes per expression, REF-5" is.
