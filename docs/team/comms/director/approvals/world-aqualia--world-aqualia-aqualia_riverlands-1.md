Reviewed `overview.png` and the screen crops (including 1-1). Preview art and placeholders
are not part of this decision; composition is.

- **What works:** the road net connects the village exits to the region edges, the river
  crossings have bridges, and walkability passes.

Changes requested (blender, worldforge passes):
1. **Empty screens.** Decor is 0.39 per 100 tiles. Most screens are open grass with three
   to seven objects. Each screen needs a reason to walk into it: a grove, a reed bed, a
   bank feature, a forage cluster or a landmark, per the bible's Riverlands zones.
2. **Staircase edges.** The river and diagonal roads step in hard two-tile stairs across
   whole screens. Vary the width, add bends and bank shapes (sand and reeds on the inside
   of bends), and let roads curve rather than staircase.
3. **Rectangle zones.** The brown block along the south edge and the grey square in the
   north read as untextured placeholders. Give them shapes and edges (field rows with
   hedges, quarry steps), or remove them.
4. Packet each pass so the before/after shows the change.

The approved village level is unaffected.
