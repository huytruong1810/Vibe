# Region-bible checklist

This is a checklist for a proposal, not authority to edit protected bibles.

- Identity: materials, palette, sound, geography and arrival experience.
- Level sizes and world offsets; a subject in every 30-by-17-tile screen.
- Landmarks: semantic ID, position, tolerance and supporting relationships.
- Routes: authored points, surface, water width, crossings and entrances.
- Zones: density intent, elevation, fight readability and presentation depth.
- Story spots: subject and 12-by-7-tile viewing window.
- Buildings, services, canonical residents and schedule anchors.
- Habitat, ambience, lights, music and festival routes.
- Candidate review evidence, explicit limitations and director decision record.

Follow the existing Aqualia plan format. Do not introduce new runtime meanings;
contract changes require DOC-5 and a schema version decision.
